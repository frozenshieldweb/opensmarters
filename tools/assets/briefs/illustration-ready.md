# Brief: "ready" illustration (Afterglow light-line art)

Purpose: a small illustration shown when installation has finished (the "ready" screen)
and on the website's download moment. It must match the project's existing empty-state
illustrations: **luminous line art drawn with light** on pure black.

Generate a **square** image (1254×1254) on a **pure black background (#000000)**. It is
converted to transparency afterwards, so the black must be clean and flat everywhere.

Style (match exactly):
- Thin, even strokes that look **drawn with light**: champagne (#E6BE91) to cream (#F4ECE4)
  to muted rose (#C9868A) along each stroke, with a close, soft halation around the lines.
  No fills and no solid shapes.
- A shared **gently bowed horizon arc** across the lower third, the same device the other
  illustrations use.
- Generous empty space: the motif occupies about 55% of the frame, centred, resting on the
  horizon.

Motif: a **5:4 rounded screen frame whose top-right and bottom-left corners are left open**
(two L-shaped strokes facing each other), standing on the horizon. A **small four-point
spark** glows just outside the open top-right corner, as if the screen had just lit up.
Inside the frame, one soft rising arc of light suggests something about to begin. The frame
is open, calm and welcoming, not a technical diagram.

Strictly avoid: text, letters, numbers, check marks, logos, play buttons, arrows, devices
with stands or buttons, people, colour outside the champagne-cream-rose range, gradients in
the background, noise or grain in the black.
