# Brief: installer side panel (Afterglow)

Purpose: the right-hand art panel of a small desktop installer window (the panel is
300×460 points, so the image is a tall portrait). The left part of the window is a calm
dark card with text; this art sits beside it and must feel like the quiet warmth left in a
cinema after the picture fades.

Generate a **portrait 2:3** image (1024×1536).

Direction, same family as the project's approved "Afterglow" key art:
- A deep warm near-black room (like #141013). About 60% of the frame stays dark and calm.
- Two or three **softly bowed, translucent light planes**, like thin sheets of frosted
  glass seen edge-on, curving gently and rising **diagonally from the lower right toward the
  upper left**, as if light were flowing upward and settling.
- A **luminous champagne rim light** (#E6BE91) along the edges of the planes, melting into a
  **muted rose halation** (#C9868A) where they overlap. The brightest highlight is at most
  ~85% brightness, and is soft rather than a hard specular.
- The **left 30% of the frame is darkest** (it will fade into the dark window surface);
  the light mass sits in the right and upper half.
- A fine, even film grain. No mottling, no specks, no stars, no bokeh dots.

Strictly avoid: text, letters, numbers, logos, symbols, screens, devices, televisions,
people, faces, hands, landscapes, recognisable objects, lens flares, neon, blue/teal/purple
casts, hard geometric outlines, and anything that resembles a brand.
